Flip Reader Upload

Send a book or a sleep screen picture to the e-paper reader over Bluetooth.

This browser has no Web Bluetooth

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer or an Android phone. On an iPhone, use the Bluefy browser (Safari does not support Web Bluetooth).

1. On the reader

  1. Double press: menu.
  2. Short presses to Upload (BLE), hold to choose.
  3. The screen shows "Upload" and the green light is on.

2a. Send a book

2b. Send a sleep screen picture

Preview as the reader shows it: 122 x 250 pixels, black and white (dithered). The reader shows it after 2 minutes asleep, or when the button is held 3 s.

Removes the stored picture: the reader shows its standard sleep screen again (title, moon, reading progress).
